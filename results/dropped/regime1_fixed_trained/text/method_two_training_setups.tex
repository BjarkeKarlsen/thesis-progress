Two training setups are reported, and they answer different questions. % [A56] [A9]
In the first, each controller is trained under fixed storage only, and the
one frozen policy then meets every storage rule. The gain then measures the
storage rule for that policy, and this is the main result. In the second,
each storage rule gets its own policy, trained under the same rule it is
evaluated with. The gain then compares two whole systems, storage rule and
policy together, and part of it can come from training. This setup is also
harder to learn, because demand and congestion-aware storage change which % [A108]
shelves the picks come from, through \nref{eq:coupling}, while the policy is
learning (Section~\ref{sec:pf:coupling}).

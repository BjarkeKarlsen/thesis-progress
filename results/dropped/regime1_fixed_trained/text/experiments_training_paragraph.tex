
\paragraph{Training.} Each learned controller is trained with PPO under
the same budget of environment steps, using the values in
Table~\ref{tab:trainparams}. Both training regimes of
Section~\ref{sec:method:training} are run. In the first the controller
trains under fixed storage and is evaluated under all three storage
rules. In the second it trains under the storage rule it is evaluated
with. The main result uses the first. Training seeds and evaluation % [A56]
seeds are counted apart. The spread over training seeds shows how much the
result depends on the trained policy, and the spread over evaluation seeds
how much it depends on the order stream.

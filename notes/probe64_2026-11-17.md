# The 64-warehouse probes (written 2026-11-17)

Question (author, 2026-11-16): does the decentralised arm get relatively better in a bigger warehouse?

## What ran

The fleet 16 policies trained under fixed storage, played without training again. 3 test seeds
(200000 to 200002), 4000 steps with a 1000-step warm-up, sampled actions, GPU.

| probe | agents | warehouse | load | section arm | folder |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, transfer | 64 | fleet 64 | 0.6 | 12 sections | `~/thesis_runs/scaling64_2026-11-16/` (`scaling64_served_2026-11-16/` re-scores it with the served share, same episodes) |
| B, graph size alone | 16 | fleet 64 | 0.18 (the fleet 16 load) | 3 sections | `~/thesis_runs/wh64_agents16_2026-11-16/` |

Decision times are not clean, the probes shared the PC with the replays.

## What to read

The pick served share is not usable (review_served_share_lines.pdf), and the throughput counts only
tasks released in the window. The put-aways do not have that fault, so they are the fair
comparison here. Means over 3 seeds.

## What it says

1. **No.** The decentralised arm gets relatively worse, not better. At fleet 16 it serves about a
   third as many put-aways as the section-based arm. At 64 agents it serves almost none (0.01
   against 0.30), and its backlog grows fastest.
2. **The bigger warehouse alone does the harm.** With 16 agents in the 64 warehouse (probe B) the
   decentralised arm and the arm with messages already finish no put-aways. More agents (probe A)
   make it worse but are not needed for the failure.
3. **Locks everywhere.** Every arm has two agents holding one task for more than 500 steps. For the
   decentralised arm the lock lasts almost the whole run (3970 of 4000 steps), as at fleet 16.
4. **Section-based holds up best**, as at fleet 16. It keeps about half its fleet 16 put-away share.

## Limits

- Transfer only. The policies never trained in a warehouse this size. A policy trained at 64 could
  behave differently, and the fleet 32 training on Colab is the first step towards checking that.
- 3 seeds and 4000 steps, no intervals. Read it as a direction, not a result.
- Fleet 16 column partly from 6 seeds.
